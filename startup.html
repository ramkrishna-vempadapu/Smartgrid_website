Company Profile

From Energy Visibility to Intelligent Action

Power Sense Pvt. Ltd. is a deep-tech spin-off company originating from applied research at the Smart Grid R&D Center, IIT Hyderabad. We engineer robust hardware and cloud-connected IoT systems that empower institutional campuses, industrial facilities, and utilities to monitor, analyze, and optimize electrical energy in real time.

By bridging precision electrical measurement with edge computing and cloud analytics, Power Sense eliminates energy blind spots, prevents costly downtime, and provides actionable intelligence for sustainable power management.

“Transforming raw electrical parameters into clear, continuous, and automated energy intelligence.”
