Core Testbeds

Smart Grid Power System

An integrated laboratory platform for studying renewable generation, energy storage, power conversion, grid interaction, measurement, control and load management. Microgrid 1 and Microgrid 2 can operate independently or as an interconnected system.

Three-Phase Generation

Storage & Conversion

Energy Storage & Power Conversion

Microgrid 1 integrates Li-ion battery and supercapacitor storage with DC-DC and bidirectional buck-boost converters. Microgrid 2 includes a three-phase inverter for AC generation interfaces.

🔋

Li-ion Battery

Lithium-ion battery energy storage for charge/discharge management, backup supply and stability research in Microgrid 1.

⚡

Supercapacitor

Supercapacitor storage for high-power transient support, rapid charge/discharge cycles and energy buffering in Microgrid 1.

🔄

DC-DC Conversion

DC-DC converter units for voltage regulation and power routing between generation, storage and the DC bus.

↔️

Bidirectional Buck-Boost

Bidirectional buck-boost converters enabling controlled power flow between storage and the DC bus in both charge and discharge directions.

🔁
Three-Phase Inverter
DC to three-phase AC conversion interface for grid-connected operation in Microgrid 2.
