About the Smart Grid R&D Center

A research, demonstration and training facility focused on building awareness and practical understanding of modern energy and smart-grid technologies.

Our Objective

Purpose of the Center

The Smart Grid R&D Center provides a platform for technical demonstrations, workshops and hands-on exposure to modern energy and smart-grid technologies. The center is intended to connect academic learning with practical laboratory experience and create awareness among students, academic groups and industry-oriented visitors.

01

Technical Workshops

Conduct workshops and technical sessions on modern energy and smart-grid technologies.

02

Student Awareness

Provide students with exposure to real laboratory systems and practical energy applications.

03

Academic Engagement

Support academic departments and institutions in understanding emerging energy technologies.

04

Hands-on Demonstration

Provide practical demonstrations using real microgrid and energy-system infrastructure.

05

Industry Exposure

Help visitors understand practical and industry-oriented applications of modern power and energy technologies.

06

Knowledge Exchange

Create opportunities for interaction, learning and awareness through demonstrations and technical programs.
