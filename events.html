Events & Demonstrations

Experience the Smart Grid R&D Center through guided laboratory demonstrations, technical workshops and capacity-building programmes.

DEMONSTRATION FORMATS

Structured Demonstration Formats

To provide tailored educational and technical experiences, the Smart Grid R&D Center offers structured laboratory demonstrations in three formats.

5
MINUTES

5 Minutes — Quick Introduction

A concise introduction to the Smart Grid R&D Center, its laboratory environment and major facilities.

Suitable for:
  • Short visits
  • General visitors
  • Orientation sessions
15
MINUTES

15 Minutes — Standard Demonstration

A guided demonstration introducing the laboratory facilities, monitoring systems and selected applications.

Suitable for:
  • Academic visitors
  • Researchers
  • Faculty groups
  • Industry visitors
30
MINUTES

30 Minutes — Comprehensive Demonstration

An extended laboratory demonstration covering multiple facilities and selected Smart Grid applications.

Suitable for:
  • Technical groups
  • Training programmes
  • Research visitors
  • Professional delegations
